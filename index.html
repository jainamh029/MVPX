Methodology

Construction rules & rebalancing
⚖️
Conviction Weighting
Weights reflect research conviction, not market cap. Higher-conviction names carry more weight to concentrate exposure to the strongest structural tailwinds.
🔄
Quarterly Rebalance
The index resets to target weights at each quarter-end (QE). Positions drift with price intra-quarter, then snap back on rebalance.
🏆
Conviction Tiers
Three tiers: High (~8.3%), Medium (5.0%), and Tactical (2.5%). Tiers express the strength of each name's structural sports-economy exposure.
📊
Real Data
All prices come from Yahoo Finance via yfinance (adjusted close). A name needs ≥ 80% data coverage to be included; remaining weights renormalise.
